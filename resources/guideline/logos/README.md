The OpenGuild logo files. All text in the lockups is converted to outlines, so no font is needed to display them.

- `openguild-logo-color.svg`: the full lockup in navy `ink` with the amber keystone. For `surface` and `surface-soft`. Not for dark grounds.
- `openguild-logo-white.svg`: the full lockup with white pillars and text and the amber keystone. For `surface-dark` and dark photos.
- `openguild-logo-mono-navy.svg` and `openguild-logo-mono-white.svg`: single-ink lockups for one-colour use. The navy file is drawn in `#0F1B3D` and the white file in `#FFFFFF`, so they must sit on a light and a dark ground respectively.
- `openguild-icon-color.svg` and `openguild-icon-white.svg`: the keystone mark alone, for small sizes (16px minimum). The colour file is drawn in navy and amber for light grounds; the white file in white and amber for dark grounds.
- `openguild-icon-mono-navy.svg` and `openguild-icon-mono-white.svg`: single-ink marks, navy `#0F1B3D` and white `#FFFFFF`.
- `openguild-wms-cube.svg`: the open-cube product mark for OpenGuild WMS (navy and blue faces, amber open lid). For light grounds, at 48px above the product name.
- `openguild-app-icon.svg`: the white-and-amber mark on a navy rounded square, for the favicon and app icons. Export at 32, 180, 512 and 1024px.

SVG files show through `<img>` only and cannot inherit `currentColor`, so pick the file whose ink matches the ground.
